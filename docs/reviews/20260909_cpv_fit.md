## Physical controls

The convention `c_q=(x+q*dx)+i(y+q*dy)` and joint normalization `I_plus+I_minus` preserve charge-count information. Generation uses `I_plus/(I_plus+I_minus)`. Separately normalized charge PDFs are a different statistical model.

For constant amplitudes `(1+q*dx)`, 60 plus and 40 minus events, x=1 and y=dy=0, the analytic solution is `r=sqrt((Nplus/Nminus)*(eff_minus/eff_plus)); dx=(r-1)/(r+1)`.

## Applied corrections

Initial values are validated. During JAX evaluation, unphysical yields/fractions, invalid simplexes, nonpositive/nonfinite event densities or invalid joint normalization return infinite NLL. Scalars expand explicitly; efficiency and background vectors must match their charge dataset and be finite/non-negative. CPFitSession validates efficiency/veto outputs before cache construction. Each charge passes None only when its efficiency and veto are absent, enabling fixed-dynamics reuse.

Final validation: **45 tests passed in 11.33 s** (`test_cp_validation`, `test_cp_workflow`, `test_cp_likelihood`, `test_cp_multi_background`, `test_coefficients`, `test_minimizer`). Diff checks and lint of the new tests passed.

## Configuration limits

Relative charge counts are informative in this model. Detection asymmetry belongs in acceptance; artificially fixing generated charge counts can change the statistical interpretation. Scale/phase references should remove redundancies without fixing physical asymmetry unnecessarily. The inspected CPV notebook 26 fixes reference x,y,dy and leaves dx free. Local convergence does not certify quadrature precision or absence of other minima.
