# QMI2D review — 2026-09-09

Historical inspection of `dynamics/qmi2d.py`, DalitzAmplitude, caches and FitSession. Tests ran in separate two-core CPU processes. Applied corrections follow the findings.

## 1. Free parameters in inactive cells

The constructor accepted free parameters where active_mask=False. In `none` mode the cell was zeroed; in linear/cubic it was replaced by an active value. The unused parameter still reached iminuit.

A 2x2 mask ((True,False),(True,True)) with `Parameter.dynamics('dead',1,owner='q')` at (0,1) retained the parameter in all three modes. Changing it to 10 gave delta NLL=0 and exactly zero gradient. This introduces a null direction independent of overall scale degeneracy.

## 2. physical_bin_mask evaluated nonphysical s12

The boundary helper was called outside [(m1+m2)^2,(M-m3)^2]. Clipping Källén radicands did not make those points physical. For D+ -> pi- pi+ pi+, s12_edges=(10,11) and s13_edges=(-100,100) returned ((True,),); edges (0,1) on both axes raised ZeroDivisionError.

The boundary formula is analytic, but intersection classification uses finitely many samples per bin. It cannot guarantee detection of arbitrarily narrow intersections.

## 3. Uniform cubic formula on nonuniform grids

Uniform Catmull–Rom used physical distance only in the current interval fraction, not in neighboring slopes. With x edges (0,1,3,6,10), y edges (0,1,2,3,4), magnitudes (1,2,4,8) constant in y and zero phases, the derivatives at x=2 were 0.9999999556 from the left and 0.6000000000 from the right. Values were continuous, derivatives were not. This does not imply incorrect derivatives with respect to free node values.

## Applied corrections

Inactive cells reject free magnitude/phase parameters; fixed placeholders remain allowed. The mask validates masses/edges, intersects bins with physical s12 support, and handles s12=0 for two massless daughters. Fully external bins return False. Documentation distinguishes an analytic boundary from sampled intersection searches.

Cubic interpolation now uses Hermite slopes based on physical center spacings with local 4x4 support. Ghost-point handling preserves previous uniform-grid Catmull–Rom values. Derivative continuity applies at interior centers, not at constant continuation outside the grid. Earlier DYNAMICS-kind and finite-edge checks remain active. Folding by min/max must correspond to the intended physical coordinate-exchange symmetry.

## Validation

Initially **24 existing tests passed in 30.76 s** (`test_qmi2d`, `test_qmi_regressions`), plus 12 scenarios: none/linear/cubic × folded on/off × component normalization on/off, with one free magnitude, one free phase, fixed NR, 25 events (seed 31), resolution 12. Cached/direct tolerances were 1e-11; gradient checks used rtol=1e-6 and atol=1e-7, with absolute errors around 1e-10–1e-9.

After corrections, **58 tests passed** across the new regressions, QMI2D, QMI regressions, amplitude caches and workflow. Coverage includes inactive/external bins, zero edges, massless limits, invalid inputs, derivatives on both axes, center values, uniform-grid equivalence and free-node gradients. These are consistency checks, not a production integration-accuracy or coverage study.
